01

A SEAFOOD ECONOMY IN TRANSITION

Aquaculture takes the lead

Farmed seafood has grown in production value.
It has led state wild catch since 2016–17.

ABOUT THE CHART

Aquaculture means farming fish and other aquatic species.
Wild catch means harvesting them from natural waters.

THE TURNING POINT

A lead that held

Aquaculture first passed state wild catch in 2011–12.
State wild catch briefly regained the lead.
From 2016–17, aquaculture stayed ahead every year.

THE RECENT SHIFT

Growth has eased from its peak

Aquaculture peaked at A$2.27 billion in 2022–23.
Its value fell 7.4% over the next two years.
It still reached A$2.10 billion in 2024–25.

02

GROWTH WAS HIGHLY CONCENTRATED

Tasmania drove the aquaculture boom

Tasmania contributed most of the increase in aquaculture value.
Its national share rose from 14% to 62%.

ABOUT THE CHART

Six of seven states and territories grew.
Western Australia was the only one to decline.

THE MAIN CONTRIBUTOR

Growth concentrated in Tasmania

By 2024–25, Tasmania accounted for
62% of Australia's aquaculture value.

THE EXCEPTION

Western Australia moved against the trend

While six states and territories grew,
Western Australia ended below its 1998–99 level.

WHAT CREATES THE VALUE?

A handful of commodities shape Australia's seafood economy

Salmonids generated about one-third of national production value.
Salmonids include salmon and trout.

THE VALUE LEADER

Salmonids stand above the rest

HIGH VALUE, LOW VOLUME

Rock lobsters punch above their weight

They contributed 16.7% of production value,
despite representing only 3.7% of total quantity.

THE BIGGEST CLIMB

Salmonids rose to the top

RANK FELL, VALUE ROSE

Rock lobsters stayed highly valuable

FIXED 2023 CASE STUDY · Small Pelagic Fishery
These findings stay fixed as you explore other selections.

THE RECENT PEAK

Small Pelagic Fishery surged in 2023

Landings reached 23,067 tonnes in 2023.
This was its highest recorded total across 2011–2023.

WHAT MADE UP THE CATCH?

Two mackerel species dominated

Blue and Jack Mackerel together contributed about 91%
of Small Pelagic Fishery landings in 2023.

FROM CATCH TO CONDITION

Catch volumes alone do not reveal stock health.
Biological assessments show a different side of the story.

THE RECOVERY PATH

Five stocks moved out of overfished status

They were overfished in 2004,
but classified not overfished in 2023.
Examples include southern bluefin tuna and eastern orange roughy.

PRESSURE PERSISTED

Nine stocks remained overfished

They were classified overfished at all three checkpoints.
This group includes school shark.

FROM CONDITION TO SPACE

The reported footprint contracted
